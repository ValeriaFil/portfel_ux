About the project

A website for a small production studio specializing in video content creation, design, and promotion. The project included developing the structure, selecting the visual style, and building a responsive layout.

Concept

Video works lead the homepage — the studio’s craft is the first thing you see.

The studio focuses on video content, so video works were placed on the homepage as the main visual element. The portfolio was transformed into short video presentations, and real photos of the creators were used to increase user trust.

The Website

Homepage — video-first hero and clear service structure

Services & contact — structured, scannable sections

About — real photos of the creators build trust

Portfolio — works presented as short video pieces
